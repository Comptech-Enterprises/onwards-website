A New Standard for EnterpriseDesign & Build

DESYN by Table Space is our enterprise-focused Design & Build platform, created to move beyond the industry’s fragmented, project-led delivery model.

We built DESYN to solve a critical challenge faced by global enterprises operating in India: the lack of consistency and predictability while scaling across multiple geographies. Instead of managing multiple disconnected vendors across cities, DESYN offers a unified delivery partner, ensuring your design intent, cost transparency, and delivery timelines remain consistent, no matter where you expand.
