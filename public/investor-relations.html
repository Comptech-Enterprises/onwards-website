Company Overview

At Tablespace Technologies Limited, we are dedicated to creating long-term value for our investors through sustainable growth, operational excellence, and innovation in managed office. Details of Business

Table Space designs, builds and operates Grade-A managed office spaces for enterprises across India — Custom Built, ready-to-move-in Suites, design-and-build and Enterprise Services — across 9 cities and 80+ centres, serving 425+ enterprise clients.
