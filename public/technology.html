— The platform in your pocket

The Table Space App

Meet the Table Space App, bringing everyday workplace actions into one intuitive platform. Employees can book Meeting Rooms, Training Rooms, and Board Rooms across Table Space centres, pre-register visitors for a smoother arrival, and raise service requests with clear visibility on progress.

Built around these core modules, its Experience Capabilities make every workday more intelligent, flexible, and connected. SpaceAI, the AI-powered workspace assistant supports key workplace tasks while learning how your organisation works over time. Geo-enabled workspace discovery, and curated events and experiences bring greater ease, access, and more of the Table Space ecosystem within reach.

Core Features

  • Browse locations, view live availability, and select the right space
  • Secure your preferred time slot using membership credits
  • Enhance your booking with extending invite to external attendees and add-ons available for curated F&B services

Experience Capabilities

  • Access an AI-powered workspace assistant for booking rooms, inviting visitors, and managing service requests
  • Built to evolve, SpaceAI learns how your organisation works, making every interaction faster and more intuitive
